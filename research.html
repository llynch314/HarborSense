What is the Purpose of the Site?

The purpose of the HarborSense website is to present an innovative solution for monitoring ocean pollution using smart sensor technology. The site is designed to inform users about environmental challenges and demonstrate how real-time data can help communities better understand and respond to pollution.

What Do We Want the Website to Accomplish?

The website aims to raise awareness about ocean pollution, educate users about its causes and effects, and promote HarborSense as a practical solution. It also encourages engagement by providing interactive elements such as maps, data visualizations, and a community survey.

Who Is the Target Audience?

The primary audience includes coastal community members, marina operators, environmental advocates, students, and researchers interested in ocean health and pollution monitoring.

What Problem Does It Solve?

Many coastal areas lack affordable, real-time tools to monitor water quality. Pollution from runoff, storms, and marine traffic often goes undetected until significant damage has already occurred. HarborSense addresses this gap by providing a low-cost, sensor-based monitoring system that gives communities early warnings and actionable data.

We collected survey responses from students, coastal residents, environmental professionals, and marina operators to better understand concerns about ocean pollution and interest in HarborSense technology.

Key Findings

  • Most participants expressed strong concern about pollution and water safety.
  • Real-time alerts and visual dashboards were the most requested HarborSense features.
  • Most respondents supported having HarborSense deployed in their community.
  • Students represented the largest survey group, followed by coastal residents.
  • Many respondents preferred a pricing model under $100 or between $100–$500.
  • Environmental professionals highlighted the importance of combining pollution tracking with safety alerts.
